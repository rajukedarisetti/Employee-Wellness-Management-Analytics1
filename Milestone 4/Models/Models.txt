MODELS USED in Employee Wellness Management & Analytics (Mood Mentor)

-   langdetect Identifies the language used in a journal entry before
    further NLP processing. Helps the application support multilingual
    employee wellness inputs.

-   Google Translate (deep-translator) Converts non-English journal
    entries into English for consistent analysis. Provides a common
    language format for the sentiment and emotion models.

-   spaCy Performs multilingual sentence splitting, tokenization, and
    lemmatization. Prepares and normalizes text for the downstream NLP
    analysis pipeline.

-   VADER (vaderSentiment) Performs rule-based sentiment analysis on the
    processed journal text. Classifies the overall sentiment as
    Positive, Negative, or Neutral.

-   BERT – bhadresh-savani/bert-base-go-emotion Uses a transformer-based
    model to identify emotions from journal entries. The detected
    emotions are mapped to the app’s 6 categories: Happy, Sad, Stress,
    Angry, Fear, and Neutral.

-   Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct (local) Powers the Wellness Chat
    conversational assistant. Generates supportive wellness responses
    based on the user’s messages.
